Privacy Policy

Effective Date: November 25, 2026

SmartData Creator Studio ("we", "our", or "the Application") respects your privacy and handles creator data with strict minimal-privilege security.

1. Information We Access

When you connect your TikTok account via TikTok Login Kit, we request only the minimum scopes needed to operate:

  • user.info.basic: Basic profile display name, username, and avatar to identify the connected creator inside the studio interface.
  • video.upload and video.publish: Used solely to upload and publish video files and captions explicitly initiated by the authenticated creator.

2. How We Store & Protect Credentials

OAuth access tokens and refresh tokens are stored locally on the creator's own device with restricted owner-only file permissions (0600) and are never sold, shared, or transmitted to third parties other than official TikTok API endpoints (open.tiktokapis.com).

3. Data Deletion & Revocation

You can disconnect your account and delete stored tokens locally at any time, or revoke SmartData Creator Studio's permissions directly inside the TikTok app under Settings and privacy > Security and permissions > Apps and services permissions.
